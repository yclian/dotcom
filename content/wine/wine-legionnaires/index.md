# wine legionnaires

Wine Legionnaires is an independent tasting collective dedicated to blind comparative flights, terroir archetypes, and deep viticultural investigation. 

Flights are constructed around rigorous parameters: balanced flights, strict flight sequencing, and blind sensory evaluation to eliminate label bias and historical prestige drift.
